Mowing stripes

Option B

Topographic contours

Elevation lines drifting across the section, the way a survey map reads. It is the most literal possible answer to "landscape" and it flatters the two things the business actually sells — grading and terrain. On the dark register the same lines glow faintly, which reads as a contour map lit from below rather than a repeat of the starfield.

Option D

Aurora wash + film grain

Large, very soft colour blooms drifting under the content — sage and gold on the light register, forest and ember on the dark — with a fine film grain over the top so the gradients never band and the whole surface feels like paper rather than screen. This is the option that finally cashes in the name: the dark sections stop being flat black and start looking like sky.

Option E

Botanical linework

Oversized single-weight line drawings — fern, branch, grass blades — bleeding off the section edges at very low opacity, like a botanical plate printed under the type. Warmer and more human than the geometric options, and it gives each section a chance to carry a different plant so no two backgrounds repeat exactly.
