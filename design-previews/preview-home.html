Why homeowners pick us

Four things worth knowing before you call anyone

01020304
01

The rating is the whole pitch

4.9 stars across 53 Google reviews, earned one job at a time. Read them yourself — we would rather you did that than take our word for it.

02

Weekly means weekly

We do not run bi-weekly mowing. Two weeks of Spokane growth means cutting off too much blade at once, and it is worse for the lawn. Set routes, same day, every week.

03

Family-owned and local

Damien Barton started Supernova here in Spokane. You are dealing with the people doing the work, not a call centre in another state.

04

One crew, most of the yard

Mowing, irrigation, installs, clean-ups and snow. It means the crew who mow your lawn are the ones who notice the sprinkler head that stopped turning.

A Spokane year

What we are doing, and when

Landscaping here is seasonal work with narrow windows. Miss the overseeding window or the blow-out window and it costs you — either a thin lawn next spring or a split irrigation line.
