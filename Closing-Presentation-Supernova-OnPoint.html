Spencer's Strategies04 · OnPoint
Now — OnPoint Trailer Repair

Here's the part that surprised me: OnPoint's problem is worse — and easier to fix.

The OnPoint site is genuinely well written. Real job photos, specific service copy, a working tap-to-call link. It's better raw material than most sites I audit. But three leftover settings are cancelling all of it:

Critical · Every page
"Do not index"
A hidden instruction tells Google to skip all 3 pages. A search for "trailer repair Spokane" cannot return your site. At all.
Critical · Every share
"M&S Contractors"
Post your link on Facebook or text it to a customer, and the preview shows another company's name — a template leftover.
Critical · Every phone
Zoom disabled
Customers can't pinch-to-zoom your address, map, or number on the device nearly all of them use.
The bottom lineYou're currently paying for a website that Google has been told to ignore. Every month it stays this way, "trailer repair near me" searches in Spokane go to somebody else.
