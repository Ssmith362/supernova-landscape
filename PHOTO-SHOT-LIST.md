# Photo shot list — Supernova Landscape

The site claims, on the homepage, that **every photograph is Supernova's own work — no stock imagery.** That claim is worth keeping, so nothing here should be filled with stock or AI-generated images. These are gaps to shoot, not gaps to buy.

Ordered by what it costs the site to leave them unshot.

---

## 1. Irrigation — a working system. **Highest priority.**

**The problem.** There is no photograph of a working irrigation system anywhere in the library. The only irrigation image, `public/images/services/irrigation.jpg`, is a broken head spraying across a muddy bed full of dandelions. That is exactly the right picture for a *repair* page — it shows the fault being diagnosed — and exactly the wrong picture for a hero or a homepage panel, where it reads as "this landscaper's yards are full of weeds."

**The consequence today.** The Irrigation slide in the homepage hero rail borrows `projects/striped-lawn-acreage.jpg`. That photo makes the irrigation argument well — deep green striped lawn in high summer with scorched brown pasture right behind the fence — but it is also `aeration-overseeding`'s service image, so it now appears twice on the homepage. It is the only cross-service photo reuse on the page and it should not be permanent.

**Shoot:**

- Heads running over green turf, **early morning or golden hour**, side-lit so the water catches the light. Landscape orientation, 2000px wide minimum.
- Wide enough to include the lawn the system is actually keeping alive — the water is the subject, the healthy turf is the proof.
- One frame with a *dry, unirrigated* area in the background if you can find it. That contrast is the whole sales argument in a Spokane August.
- A controller/valve box being worked on, hands in frame. Useful for the repair section on the service page.

**Avoid:** weeds, dandelions, bare mud, midday overhead sun.

`projects/backyard-reset-after.jpg` is doing double duty on the homepage: it is the hero rail's Landscape Installs slide *and* the "after" in the before/after slider further down the page. It works, but the same yard twice on one screenful is thin.

## 5. Orientation note for anything destined for the hero

The hero is now full-bleed, so it needs **landscape** source at **2000px wide or better**. Two of the service photos are portrait phone shots (`services/irrigation.jpg` and `services/dethatching.jpg`, both 748×1000). Portrait works well in the services ribbon, where panels are tall — it is only the hero that rules them out.

When shooting for the hero specifically: leave clean, uncluttered space on the **left third** of the frame. That is where the headline sits, and a busy left side is what forces a heavy scrim and mutes the photograph.

---

## Practical notes

- Phone cameras are fine. Modern phone photos at full resolution comfortably exceed 2000px. The failure mode in this library is not camera quality, it is images that were exported or messaged at reduced size.
- Shoot in the hour after sunrise or before sunset. Every strong photo already in the library was shot in low, warm light; every weak one was shot at midday or under overcast.
- Send originals, not screenshots or messaging-app copies. `AirDrop → Original` or email at "Actual Size".
